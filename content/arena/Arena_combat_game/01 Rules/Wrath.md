# Wrath

Wrath is one track per warband that does two jobs: it sets your activations next round, and you spend it on Wrath options and Wrath spends. Spending Wrath now therefore costs you tempo later. That trade-off is the core tension.

Every warband starts at Wrath 4.

| Trigger | Wrath |
| --- | --- |
| An attack you make scores no hits | +1 |
| One of your fighters is taken out | +2 |
| You have the lowest Glory at the end of a round (ties all gain) | +1 |
| You resolve Defiance or Bleed (Wrath options that gain Wrath) | +1 or +2, as printed |
| You resolve a Wrath option that costs Wrath | −1 or −2, as printed |

The killer gains nothing on the Wrath track. Taking a fighter out is rewarded with Glory instead. This is deliberately different from Gorechosen: Wrath goes to whoever is behind, so it works as a catch-up meter and not a snowball.

**Wrath spends**

Any warband can spend Wrath on these, as well as on the Wrath options printed on Action cards:

| Spend | Cost | When | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Rage | 1 | Before rolling any attack, Ripostes included | +1 Attack die. Once per attack |
| Shake Off | 1 | At the start of one of your fighters' activations | Remove 1 Fatigue from it |
| Seize | 2 | When any Initiative card is drawn, before its owner acts | Activate one of your fighters first. Once per round |

- **Spending floor.** You can't spend Wrath below 2. Initiative never drops below 2 cards, so Wrath spent below 2 would cost nothing.
- **Seize is a normal activation:** it needs a card option or a Basic Action, and a fighter's repeat activation still gains Fatigue.
- **A slain warband can't spend Wrath.** Fate of the Slain keeps it at a fixed Wrath of 3.

**What a Wrath point costs.** Spent Wrath stays spent until you regain it, so one point costs one activation in every remaining round: 3 activations if spent in round 1, 2 in round 2, 1 in round 3 and none in round 4. Players who are behind regain Wrath quickly (+1 for lowest Glory each round, +2 per fighter lost, +1 per whiff), so for them a spend is cheap; for the leader it is expensive. Rage adds 0.33 damage to a 3-dice attack against Armour 4+ (a whole activation's Strike is worth 1.00), so it is a round 4 or finishing-blow tool. Seize is the spend worth making early: acting before a rival can take an objective or a kill first.

**Gaining Wrath from cards.** Defiance and Bleed only work for a player who is behind: anyone tied for the Glory lead counts as leading, so nobody can use them in round 1 before any Glory is scored. That stops Wrath farming, which would cost the leader nothing and lengthen every round: each extra Wrath point is one more activation in every later round.

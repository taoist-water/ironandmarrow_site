# Action Cards

All players draw from one shared, faction-neutral deck of 40 cards: 8 designs, 5 copies each. Each card offers three options, and you resolve one. Warband identity comes from fighter traits, not cards, which keeps the game miniature-agnostic.

**Riposte** is the only reaction. Play a card showing Riposte when one of your fighters is attacked in melee. After the attack resolves, if that fighter is still standing, it Strikes back with 1 fewer die.

## Starter list (first draft)

| Card | Option 1 | Option 2 | Option 3 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Advance | Move 3 | Strike | Guard |
| Lunge | Move 1, then Strike | Precise Strike (re-roll misses) | Riposte |
| Brutal Swing | Move 2 | Heavy Strike (+1 die) | Fury Strike (−1 Wrath: +2 dice) |
| Sweep | Move 2 | Cleave (every enemy in front arc, −1 die each) | Shove (push 1 hex) |
| Sidestep | Move 2, turn freely | Turn, then Strike | Riposte |
| Hold the Line | Guard | Strike | Recover (remove 1 Fatigue) |
| Bloodied Fury | Move 3 | Taunt (enemies must target this fighter next) | Fury Strike (−2 Wrath: +2 dice, crits on 5+) |
| Opportunist | Move 2 | Backstab (rear arc only, +2 dice) | Recover |

Move is in hexes. Strike uses the fighter's own Attack dice. Every card that offers a Fury option also offers a non-Fury option, so a player low on [[Wrath]] still has a usable card.

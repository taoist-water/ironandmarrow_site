# Scoring and Victory

The player with the most Glory after round 4 wins. If Glory is tied, the player who lost fewer fighters wins.

**Last one standing.** If only one warband has fighters left, it wins at once, whatever the Glory. Until then, a wiped-out warband plays on through Fate of the Slain and can still win on points.

| Source | Glory |
| --- | --- |
| Take out an enemy fighter | 1 |
| Bounty: the fighter taken out belonged to the Glory leader (ties count) | +1 |
| End of round: each objective hex you hold (your fighter on it, not Exhausted) | 1 |
| Arena Challenge: one public card revealed each round, first to meet it | 1 |

**Fate of the Slain.** A warband with no fighters left keeps adding Initiative cards at a fixed Wrath of 3. When one of its cards is drawn, the player picks one Spite action:

- Place a hazard token on an empty hex. Any fighter entering it takes 1 damage.
- Give any fighter 1 Fatigue.
- Roll 2 Attack dice at any fighter. Normal saves apply.

A Spite action that takes out a fighter scores Glory as normal.

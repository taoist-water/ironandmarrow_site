# Concept and Pillars

A 2–4 player arena skirmish game: small warbands of 3–4 fighters fight on a hex arena for Glory over 4 rounds, using any miniatures the players own. Target play time is 60–75 minutes at four players.

## Design pillars

1. **Every activation is a tense choice.** You never know exactly when you act next, and every card you play is three options you can't use later.
2. **Losing players stay dangerous.** Setbacks feed [[Wrath]], and Wrath buys activations and big attacks.
3. **Any models, any setting.** Fighters are built from archetype chassis and traits, not tied to a model range. See [[Fighters and Warbands]].
4. **Multiplayer without kingmaking.** Scoring rewards hitting the leader, and wiped-out players keep playing. See [[Scoring and Victory]].

## What each source contributes

| Source | Taken | Left behind |
| --- | --- | --- |
| SAGA | To-hit against Armour, flat saves, Fatigue as a brake on repeat activations | Battle Boards, SAGA dice, unit-based casualties, mutual melee |
| Warhammer Underworlds | Hex board, small named warbands, Glory scoring, Guard and Charge | Per-warband card decks, deckbuilding, the 1v1 board |
| Gorechosen | Wrath-weighted initiative deck, multi-option Action cards, facing arcs, Critical Injuries, Fate of the Slain, 2–4 player arena | Single champions, Wrath awarded for kills, last-one-standing victory |

# Fighters and Warbands

Every fighter is a chassis plus up to two traits, bought from a 12-point budget, and a warband is 3–4 fighters. The models are only stand-ins, so any miniature works.

## Chassis (first-draft costs)

| Chassis | Move | Attack | Armour | Wounds | Cost | Role |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Brute | 3 | 4 | 4+ | 5 | 4 | Hits hard, soaks damage |
| Defender | 3 | 2 | 5+ | 4 | 4 | Holds objectives |
| Leader | 4 | 3 | 4+ | 4 | 4 | While alive: hand size +1 |
| Duelist | 4 | 3 | 4+ | 4 | 3 | All-rounder |
| Marksman | 3 | 2 (ranged 3, range 5) | 3+ | 3 | 3 | Ranged pressure |
| Skirmisher | 5 | 2 | 3+ | 3 | 2 | Objective grabber, flanker |

A warband can include at most one Leader and one Brute.

## Traits (+1 point each, max 2 per fighter)

| Trait | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Swift | +1 Move |
| Tough | +1 Wound |
| Reach | Melee Kill Zone extends 2 hexes into the front arc |
| Shielded | Saves on 3+ when Guarded |
| Cleaver | Cleave without losing a die |
| Relentless | First repeat activation each round gains no Fatigue |
| Unshakeable | Cannot be Shoved |

**Footprint.** Fighters on bases up to about 40 mm take 1 hex. Large models take 3 hexes, cost +2, and cannot take Swift.

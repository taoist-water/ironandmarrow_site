# Fatigue

Fatigue stops a player from spending every activation on their best fighter. It is taken directly from SAGA, where opponents spend your Fatigue against you.

**Gaining Fatigue.** A fighter gains 1 Fatigue when it activates for the second or later time in a round, and when it resolves a Charge (any option that moves and then Strikes, such as Lunge).

**Spending Fatigue.** Any opponent fighting a Fatigued fighter can remove 1 of its Fatigue tokens to do one of the following:

- When attacking it: its Armour is 1 worse for that attack.
- When it attacks: it rolls 1 fewer Attack die.

**Exhausted.** At 3 Fatigue a fighter is Exhausted. It can only take Move 1 or Recover, and it cannot hold objectives.

**Removing Fatigue.** The Recover option on an [[Action Cards|Action card]] removes 1. Each fighter removes 1 at the end of every round.

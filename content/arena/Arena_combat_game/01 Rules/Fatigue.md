# Fatigue

Fatigue stops a player from spending every activation on their best fighter. It is taken directly from SAGA, where opponents spend your Fatigue against you.

**Gaining Fatigue.** A fighter gains 1 Fatigue when it activates for the second or later time in a round, and when it resolves a Charge (Advance or Lunge). It also gains 1 Fatigue after making a Riposte. Enemies can give it Fatigue with Harry, War Cry or a blocked Shove.

**Spending Fatigue.** Opponents spend a fighter's Fatigue by removing its tokens. Each token removed does one of these:

- **When attacking it:** its Armour is 1 worse for that attack.
- **When it attacks:** it rolls 1 fewer Attack die, or the target's Armour is 1 better for that attack.
- **When it moves** (a Move, Step or Charge): it moves 1 hex less. Any number of tokens can be spent on one move, by any opponents, in seat order clockwise from its owner.

**Attacker first.** Any number of tokens can be spent, on an attack as well as on a move. In an attack, if both fighters have Fatigue, the attacker spends first, then the defender. A natural 6 still always hits and a natural 1 always misses, whatever the Armour.

**Which defensive spend to use.** Per token, against a 3-dice attack, removing a die is better for a defender with Armour 3+ (1.33 damage down to 0.89, against 1.00) and also cuts Critical Injuries from 37% to 25%. Raising Armour is better at 5+ (0.67 down to 0.33, against 0.44). At 4+ the two are equal on damage (0.67), but removing a die still cuts Critical Injuries more.

**Exhausted.** At 3 Fatigue a fighter is Exhausted. It can only Step (even with cards in hand) or Recover, and it cannot hold objectives.

**Removing Fatigue.** The Recover option on an Action card removes 1. Each fighter removes 1 at the end of every round.

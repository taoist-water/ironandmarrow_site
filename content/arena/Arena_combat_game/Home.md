# Arena Battle Game

A 2–4 player, miniature-agnostic arena skirmish game. Small warbands of 3–4 fighters fight on a hex arena for Glory over 4 rounds. Target play time is 60–75 minutes at four players.

Built from SAGA (combat, Fatigue), Warhammer Underworlds (hex board, small warbands, Glory) and Gorechosen (Wrath-weighted initiative deck, 2–4 player arena).
